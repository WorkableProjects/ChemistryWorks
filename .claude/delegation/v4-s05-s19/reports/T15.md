## Guide text
**Game options.** With "Allow game mode" on, a Game mode options panel appears in the builder. You can choose which games students see, how many questions, seconds per question (Classic) or in total (Lightning Round, Unit Dash), lives (Survival, Boss Battle), and which power-ups exist (50/50, Freeze, Skip, Hint). Relaxed mode removes every clock and speed bonus; you can force it for everyone or let students switch it on from the game's start screen. You can also limit the question types, turn sound off, and mark the class leaderboard setting (stored now, used by a later update).
**New games.** Tower (right answer = block, wrong = wobble, three wobbles and it falls), Boss Battle (harder questions hit harder, wrong answers let the boss hit back), Mistake Review (replays a student's missed questions until each is right twice; not scored) and Unit Dash (made-up unit conversions and sig-fig rounding, works on any test). Mistake Review only unlocks after a student has missed something in a game on that device.
