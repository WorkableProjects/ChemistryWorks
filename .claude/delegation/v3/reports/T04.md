## Design notes
- categorize: each item card has a `<select>` (works without drag); native HTML5 drag onto a group is an optional extra (desktop only; no touch drag).
- hotspot: uses the question-level `image` field (builder's existing Image field) as the diagram. x,y are fractions of width/height; distance is Euclidean in those fractions with `radius` (default 0.06), so on non-square images the area is slightly oval. Keyboard: pick label, arrows (Shift = 0.1) move crosshair, Enter places, Esc cancels, Delete removes.
- graph: SVG drawn inline. plot keyboard: arrows (Shift = 5 steps), Enter adds or removes, Delete removes last. Plot answer coordinates are snapped to about range/200. Grading matches each target to its nearest unused placed point within the tolerance box; extras (more points than targets) make it incorrect, partial = hits/targets.
- Games: Survival accepts all auto-graded types including hotspot and graph (they render fine on the board). Classic/Flashcards still use GAME_TYPES (unchanged, per task). Lightning gets only ptable and econfig.
